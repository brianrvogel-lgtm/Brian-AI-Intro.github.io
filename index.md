# Introduction to AI

## Assignment 1

### Digital Twin

#### What emerging technology did you choose? How is AI being used to support this technology?
##### Digital Twin technology allows me to develop models of the supply chain where I can create scenarios and test hypotheses without making large capital investments.  I use AI to help build and challenge these models, recommend variables and sensitivities to test, and evaluate how changes could affect key supply chain KPIs.  AI also helps me pressure-test different ideas from multiple perspectives and communicate complex modeling outputs to non-technical audiences.

#### How does the use of AI and this technology affect everyday life?
##### AI-supported Digital Twin modeling has allowed me to get to potential solutions much faster.  I use AI to review both the models and supporting presentations from the perspectives of different stakeholder groups.  This helps me anticipate questions and concerns before decisions are made and communicate recommendations more effectively.  It also helps me clearly distinguish where I am seeking input or alignment from areas that have already been decided or are considered non-negotiable.
