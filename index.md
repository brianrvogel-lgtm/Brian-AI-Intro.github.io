#### **Explain AT Least Two of the seven steps of Machine Learning as you would explain to a friend that knows nothing about Machine Learning.**
##### **Evaluation-** Using a smaller sample of your original dataset that is outside of the initial group and running it through your model to see how it responds. It's important to use a separate group of data because the original data was used to make the algorithm, and you want to understand how it would operate in the real world. Same prinicples of making sure the data in the dataset is randomized as well will ensure the model doesn't have a pattern built into it that was unintended.

** Prediction- ** Is how good your model is at delivering its intended results.  This should depend on the situation: a normal model that gets the answer right 80% of the time should be considered good, but a model built to assess medical conditions should have a higher level of accuracy.
